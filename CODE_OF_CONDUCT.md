# Community code of conduct

Octogee welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels.
Treat people with respect, focus criticism on ideas and code, and make room
for questions and disagreement. Give credit for others’ work and respect their
privacy.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexual harassment, doxxing, and sharing
private information without consent are not acceptable. These expectations
apply in project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.

Report concerns privately to **support@octogee.com**. Include links or other
relevant context and avoid distributing private information more widely.
Reports are handled as privately as practical. Maintainers may remove content,
limit participation, or block accounts in proportion to the behavior, and may
reconsider decisions when new information is provided. Maintainers are held
to the same standards.
